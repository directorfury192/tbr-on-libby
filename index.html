Export your StoryGraph library
How to get your CSV:
  1. Go to app.thestorygraph.com and log in
  2. Click your profile icon (top right) → Account
  3. Scroll down to Import and Export Data
  4. Click Export your library — a CSV file will download
  5. Upload that file below ↓

How to find your library key (30 seconds):
  1. Open libbyapp.com in a new tab
  2. Click Add a library and search for your library by name or zip code
  3. Once you see your library, look at the browser URL — it will say something like:
    libbyapp.com/library/dclibrary
  4. Copy just that last part (e.g. dclibrary) and paste it below
